ZAPPER POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Zapper collects information needed to operate the service, including host name and email, account status, subscription dates, session records and saved transcript records. When available, a participant Google sign-in email may be associated with a saved transcript.

How information is used

Information is used for authentication, session access, live transcription, transcript delivery, customer support, security and account administration. Participants do not need to create a Zapper website account to launch the desktop application.

Passwords and security

Account passwords are stored using one-way password hashing. Password-reset links are time limited. Customers should not share passwords and should install desktop builds only from official Zapper release links.

Retention and deletion

Zapper retains operational account and meeting data according to product configuration and legitimate service needs. Customers may contact support regarding account or privacy questions.
