ZAPPER POLICY

Billing & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Zapper offers a digital host subscription for ₹999 for 1 month, ₹2,697 for 3 months, or ₹5,095 for 6 months. The one-time free trial lasts 24 hours from registration. Users can view subscription start and end times in their Zapper host dashboard. Any applicable taxes and the final payable total should be disclosed before the customer authorizes a payment.

Fixed-term plans and discounted totals

Choose 1 month: ₹999, 3 months: ₹2,697 (10% discount, rounded), or 6 months: ₹5,095 (15% discount, rounded). A host may run separate parallel meeting sessions using the same host account on different computers; each session has its own PIN. These are one-time, fixed-term digital subscription purchases, not recurring payment mandates. Plans extend the current paid access end date when access is still active. Checkout shows the final amount before payment; activation occurs only after Cashfree confirms successful payment.

Service delivery and activation

Subscriptions grant online access to create meeting sessions for the active period. Zapper is a digital service and does not ship physical products. The service is made available after the host account is activated for the paid subscription term. See Digital Service Delivery.

Renewals

A subscription is active only for the account's recorded start and end dates. Cashfree checkout on this website does not establish a recurring card or UPI mandate. You choose whether to buy another fixed-term period. When a payment option is made available, its displayed price, duration, payment method and any renewal consent govern that transaction. The current host dashboard remains the source for account access status.

Cancellation

To cancel a future renewal (if recurring billing is enabled), contact zapperapp.support@gmail.com with your registered host email, or call +91 93460 73219. We aim to acknowledge the request within 2 business days. Where possible, request cancellation before the next billing date to avoid a renewal already in processing. Access for an already paid period normally continues until its recorded end date, subject to lawful suspension or account closure.

Refunds and payment problems

Duplicate charges, failed activation, and processing errors are addressed under our Refund Policy. Approved refunds go to the original payment method through the provider; processing time depends on the bank/provider.

Billing questions

Include the registered Zapper host email, payment date and transaction reference. Do not share passwords or full card details.
