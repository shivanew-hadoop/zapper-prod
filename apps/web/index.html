POLICIES & PAYMENT READINESS

Clear terms for using and paying for Zapper.

These sections are available throughout the site for account, payment and support transparency.

Terms of Service

Zapper provides software for meeting transcription and related host session management. Users are responsible for obtaining any consent required before recording or transcribing a meeting, safeguarding account credentials and using the service lawfully. Service availability may depend on third-party platforms, network connectivity and supported operating-system capabilities.

Paid access is licensed to the registered host account for the active subscription period. Misuse, attempts to bypass access controls, unlawful transcription, abusive traffic or interference with the service may result in suspension.

Privacy Policy

Zapper stores account details needed to operate the service, including name, email, subscription status, session records and saved transcript data. Participant Google identity may be stored with a transcript when available. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

Data is used to provide authentication, session access, transcription, support and account administration. Zapper does not require participants to create a website account. Customers should avoid entering sensitive information that is not needed for the meeting.

Billing & Cancellation

The displayed host plan is ₹1,000 per month unless a different price is presented at checkout. Subscription access starts when activated and remains available until the recorded end date. Customers may request cancellation through the support contact below. Cancellation prevents future renewal but does not automatically erase records required for service operation or lawful accounting.

Refund Policy

If a payment is duplicated, charged in error, or the paid service cannot be provided due to a verified Zapper-side issue, contact support with the account email and transaction reference. Eligible refunds are reviewed case by case and, when approved, are returned to the original payment method according to payment-provider timelines.

Usage already consumed during an active billing period may affect refund eligibility except where applicable law requires otherwise.

Security & Responsible Use

Zapper uses authenticated host accounts, time-limited application tokens and server-side session validation. Users should keep login credentials and session PINs confidential and install desktop builds only from the official Zapper release link.

Support & Grievance Contact

For account, billing, privacy, refund or technical questions, contact zapperapp.support@gmail.com. Include your registered email and a concise description of the issue. Do not send passwords or private meeting content unless specifically required for troubleshooting.
