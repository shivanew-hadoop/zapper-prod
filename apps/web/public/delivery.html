ZAPPER POLICY

Digital Service Delivery

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Zapper is an online software and meeting-transcription subscription. No physical goods are shipped and no shipping charges apply to the digital subscription.

How access is delivered

New hosts register online and receive a one-time 24-hour trial. Paid host access is enabled for the start and end times shown in the host dashboard after subscription activation. Hosts can then create sessions, issue PINs and download saved transcripts as allowed by their account plan.

Delivery timing and assistance

Digital access is normally available once the account subscription has been activated. If a paid account has not been activated, please contact support with your registered email and payment reference so we can investigate and resolve the issue.
