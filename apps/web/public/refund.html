ZAPPER POLICY

Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Zapper provides a digital subscription service, not a physical product. The standard host plan is ₹999 for 1 month, ₹2,697 for 3 months, or ₹5,095 for 6 months. Refund requests can be made using the support contact below; applicable consumer rights are not limited by this policy.

Plan prices

Published fixed-term subscription prices are ₹999 for one month, ₹2,697 for three months, and ₹5,095 for six months. Discounted totals are rounded to whole rupees. Cashfree is the payment processor and the final payable amount is displayed before you authorize payment.

When you can request a refund

If you were charged twice for the same order, charged incorrectly, or paid for access that Zapper could not activate or deliver due to a verified Zapper-side issue, please contact us within 7 calendar days of the charge, where possible. Share your registered email, payment date, payment reference and a brief explanation. Never send your password or card security code.

Partial use and cancellations

Paid subscription time already used is generally not refundable for a change of mind. If a payment error, duplicate collection or failure to deliver occurred, we will assess the unused portion and any refund required under applicable law. A cancellation request does not automatically refund the period already delivered; see our Billing & Cancellation Policy.

Review and refund processing

We aim to acknowledge a complete refund request within 2 business days and communicate a decision within 7 business days after receiving the information needed to investigate. Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method through the payment provider, where supported. Bank/payment-provider processing normally requires additional time (often 5–10 business days) and may vary.

Refund or billing support

Email zapperapp.support@gmail.com or call +91 93460 73219. For unresolved payment concerns, reply to the support thread with your case details.
