Contact & Support

Last updated: 19 September 2026

For technical support, account access, billing, privacy, cancellation or refund requests, contact Zapper using the email below.

Email

zapperapp.support@gmail.com

What to include

Include your registered host email, a short description of the issue and, for billing questions, the payment reference. Do not send your password or unrelated private meeting content.

Password help

Use Forgot password on the Zapper sign-in screen to generate a secure password-reset link. Administrators can also set a new password for a host account from the admin console.
