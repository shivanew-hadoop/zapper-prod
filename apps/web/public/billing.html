ZAPPER POLICY

Billing & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Zapper offers a digital host subscription for ₹1,000 per month. The one-time free trial lasts 24 hours from registration. Users can view subscription start and end times in their Zapper host dashboard. Any applicable taxes and the final payable total should be disclosed before the customer authorizes a payment.

Service delivery and activation

Subscriptions grant online access to create meeting sessions for the active period. Zapper is a digital service and does not ship physical products. The service is made available after the host account is activated for the paid subscription term. See Digital Service Delivery.

Renewals

A subscription is active only for the account's recorded start and end dates. Automatic card/UPI recurring collection is not enabled by this website unless explicitly presented and separately authorized at checkout. When a payment option is made available, its displayed price, duration, payment method and any renewal consent govern that transaction. The current host dashboard remains the source for account access status.

Cancellation

To cancel a future renewal (if recurring billing is enabled), contact zapperapp.support@gmail.com with your registered host email, or call +91 93460 73219. We aim to acknowledge the request within 2 business days. Where possible, request cancellation before the next billing date to avoid a renewal already in processing. Access for an already paid period normally continues until its recorded end date, subject to lawful suspension or account closure.

Refunds and payment problems

Duplicate charges, failed activation, and processing errors are addressed under our Refund Policy. Approved refunds go to the original payment method through the provider; processing time depends on the bank/provider.

Billing questions

Include the registered Zapper host email, payment date and transaction reference. Do not share passwords or full card details.
